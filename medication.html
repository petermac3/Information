Lansoprazole
Taken before breakfast Many heart patients take medications that can irritate the stomach lining or prevent blood clotting. Lansoprazole belongs to a class of drugs called proton pump inhibitors (PPIs). It reduces the amount of acid your stomach makes, protecting against gastrointestinal bleeding that can be a side effect of aggressive heart therapies
Side effects Usually mild including constipation, farting, dizzy or tired

Aspirin
A nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) used to relieve pain, reduce fevers, decrease inflammation, and prevent harmful blood clots.
Side effects. Can irritate the stomach lining, leading to indigestion, stomach ulcers, or serious gastrointestinal and brain bleeding

Bisoprolol
A prescription beta-blocker medicine used to treat high blood pressure (hypertension), chest pain (angina), and stable heart failure.
Side effects. Dizziness,headaches,tiredness, cold fingers and toes.

Clopidogrel
An antiplatelet medication that prevents blood cells from sticking together, lowering the risk of dangerous blood clots. It is commonly prescribed to prevent heart attacks and strokes in patients with a history of cardiovascular disease, acute coronary syndrome (ACS), peripheral arterial disease, or those with recent coronary
Side effects.The most common side effect is bleeding more easily—such as nosebleeds, bruising, bleeding gums,

Ramipril
A prescription ACE inhibitor primarily used to treat high blood pressure, manage heart failure, and protect the heart and kidneys after a heart attack. It relaxes blood vessels, making it easier for the heart to pump and reducing the long-term risk of strokes, heart attacks, and kidney failure
Side effects. Minor, including tiredness

Atorvastatin
Taken at Night Atorvastatin (commonly known by the brand name Lipitor). A prescription statin medication used to lower "bad" cholesterol and triglycerides in the blood, and to raise "good" cholesterol. It is primarily prescribed to prevent cardiovascular diseases, including heart attacks and strokes, in high-risk individuals.
Side effects. Minor, including farting, constipation, runny nose.
